## Proposed remediation

Migration 069 adds `QUARANTINED`, a JSON reason, and quarantine time to the
result acquisition state. On an ordered provider identity mismatch, the result
repository rechecks the immutable fixture anchor under lock, counts OPEN and
settled PLAYED picks, then records the conflicting provider IDs, team order,
kickoff, status, and payload digest. It leaves all picks, historical results,
settlements, ledger entries, KellyLab records, Research rows, and QuantLab rows
unchanged. Quarantined states are excluded from claim and due-work queries, so
worker restarts do not trigger another fetch for an unchanged conflict.

The first quarantine logs a structured warning. Operational counts and the
Research health view show quarantined results and the number with open PLAYED
picks. Other persistence conflicts retain their existing error behavior. A
score correction with unchanged fixture identity continues through the existing
confirmation and settlement correction path.
